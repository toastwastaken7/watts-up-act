Letter to a Representative

To the Honorable Scott Peters

Representative for California's 50th Congressional District and member of the House Energy and Commerce Committee, which has jurisdiction over energy policy.

October 29, 2026

The Honorable Scott Peters
U.S. House of Representatives
Washington, DC 20515

Dear Congressman Peters,

My name is Nikhil Kyadiggeri, and I am a student at Canyon Crest Academy in your district. I am writing about an issue that affects every San Diego family that pays an electric bill: the rapid growth of AI data centers. According to the Department of Energy's Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory, data centers used about 4.4 percent of all U.S. electricity in 2023 and could use up to 12 percent by 2028. When utilities build new power plants and transmission lines to serve these facilities, the cost is usually spread across every customer, so families who never use a data center end up paying for it.

In September, the House passed the Ratepayer Protection Act (H.R. 9340) by a vote of 417 to 3, but that bill only asks states to consider making data centers pay their fair share. My bill, the WATTS UP Act, goes further. It would require large data centers to publicly report their energy and water use, to pay the full cost of the grid upgrades they need, and to meet efficiency and clean energy standards over time, all funded by a small fee on the data centers themselves. The full bill and my sources are on my website: https://toastwastaken7.github.io/watts-up-act/.

As a member of the House Energy and Commerce Committee, you are in a unique position to move these ideas forward, and I would be grateful for your support. Thank you very much for your time and consideration.

Sincerely,

Nikhil Kyadiggeri
